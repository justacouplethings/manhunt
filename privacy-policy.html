Privacy Policy for Manhunt: GPS Pursuit

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Notice Regarding Location Data: Manhunt is an outdoor physical social game that requires real-time access to your device's geographical location to function. We only process location data during active game matches. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your location data.

1. Information We Collect

Manhunt collects only the minimum information necessary to provide live game functionality:

  • Precise Location Data: GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, accuracy, bearing, speed) are captured while you are in an active game session. Seekers transmit live coordinates; Runners transmit periodic snapshots (default: once every 5 minutes).
  • Ephemeral Nickname: The chosen player display name entered when creating or joining a room.
  • Game Session Metadata: Room code, assigned role (Runner or Seeker), tag events, and match timestamps.
  • Device Hardware Capabilities: Vibration hardware and Web Audio output used for proximity alerts and tactical sound cues.

2. How We Use Location Data (Google Play Policy Compliance)

Under Google Play's Location and Foreground Services policies:

  • Core Functionality: Location data is used strictly to calculate distances between players, display blips on the in-game tactical map, and determine tag distance.
  • Background Location (Optional): If granted, background location enables the app to keep your runner ping timer accurate while your phone is locked in your pocket or when switching briefly to another app during an active chase.
  • No Persistent Tracking: All location coordinates are ephemeral. Once a match ends or you disconnect from the room, your coordinates are discarded from memory and are not stored in any permanent database.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not share your personal data with any advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics corporations. Coordinate telemetry is transmitted over encrypted WebSocket/WSS protocols directly to players sharing your specific Room Code.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Manhunt does not knowingly collect or solicit personally identifiable information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. All players operate under temporary arbitrary nicknames, and no account creation, email registration, or social profile linkage is ever requested or required.

5. Outdoor Safety & User Responsibility

Physical Safety Warning: Always pay attention to your real-world surroundings while playing Manhunt. Do not run across roads, into traffic, private property, or hazardous areas. Always obey local laws, pedestrian guidelines, and park regulations.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Manhunt operates in a serverless/ephemeral architecture, no personal identification accounts or location histories are permanently retained. To reset all local client preferences (e.g. custom theme, sound toggles, ping intervals), simply tap "Clear Local Data" in the in-game Settings screen or clear your browser/app storage.

7. Permissions Requested

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: Precise outdoor GPS coordinates for accurate radar placement.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Initial network-based location triangulation.
  • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION: Allows timer snapshot updates when runner has screen locked.
  • VIBRATE: Tactical haptic pulses when a seeker is within proximity.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Prevents screen sleep during critical active search rounds.
