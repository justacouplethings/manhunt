PRIVACY POLICY

Google Play & iOS Location Privacy Commitment

Manhunt is an active outdoor GPS pursuit game. We respect your digital privacy:

  • Zero Permanent Tracking: Location coordinates are used in real-time exclusively while in an active match and are never stored on permanent databases.
  • No Ads or Data Selling: We do not include third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, or monetized data brokers.
  • Ephemeral Nicknames: No email, password, or real name registration is ever required.
  • Encrypted Transport: Room updates are transmitted over secure TLS/WSS connections directly between room participants.
